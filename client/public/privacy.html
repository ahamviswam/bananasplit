Privacy Policy

PickleTab — Last updated September 29, 2026

PickleTab ("we", "our", "the app") helps pickleball groups generate game matchups, split court fees and expenses, and track balances. This page explains what information we collect, why, and how it's handled.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: when you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and a securely hashed password (we never store your password in plain text).
  • Group and expense data: group names, member names, session details, court fees, expenses, and payment records that you or your group enter into the app.
  • Feedback: if you submit feedback through the app, we collect the message you write and, optionally, a contact email.

Guest Mode

If you use PickleTab without creating an account ("Guest Mode"), all of your data — groups, sessions, expenses, balances — is stored only on your device and is never sent to our servers. On iOS, we store the date you started your guest trial in the device Keychain so the trial period is tracked correctly even if the app is reinstalled; this timestamp contains no personal information.

How We Use Your Information

  • To operate the core features of the app — generating matchups, calculating splits, and tracking balances.
  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • To send you a password reset email if you request one.
  • To respond to feedback or support requests.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate PickleTab:

  • Railway — hosts our backend server and database.
  • Resend — delivers password reset emails on our behalf. Only the email address needed to deliver that specific email is shared with Resend.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your account and group data for as long as your account is active. To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us using the email below.

Children's Privacy

PickleTab is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
